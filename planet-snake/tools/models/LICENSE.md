# Source models and provenance

These 27 `.glb` files are offline inputs to `bake-props.py`. They are not engine
assets and deliberately live outside `assets/` because they have no
`.glb.meta.json` sidecars. They are consumed with:

    python3 tools/bake-props.py tools/models

which regenerates `src/props-data.ts`.

## Generated models — not Kenney assets

- `tree_cloudPine.glb`, `tree_cloudBroad.glb`, `tree_cloudDead.glb`, and
  `rock_cloudMossy.glb` were produced by a Hunyuan text-to-3D flow and an
  offline `cloud2prop.py` conversion. The generation prompts and conversion
  script are not included in this repository.
- `rock_pebbleCluster.glb`, `rock_slabA.glb`, and `rock_standingA.glb` were
  produced by the local Hunyuan3D asset pipeline. Their generation records are
  not included in this repository.

- `wc_dandelion.glb`, `wc_earthPillar.glb`, `wc_mushroomCap.glb`, and
  `wc_stoneRing.glb` were generated by the WorldClaw Stage 3 flow in
  `tools/plan-objects.ts`; the prompts and output paths are recorded in
  `tools/wc-stage3/grassland/objects.json`.
- `hand_claw.glb`, `hand_down.glb`, and `hand_up.glb` correspond to the generated
  candidate flow in `tools/gen-hand.ts`; the repository does not record the
  exact original-to-final filename mapping.

The repository does not contain a redistribution record for these 14 generated
outputs. Do not describe them as Kenney or CC0; confirm the model-provider and
organization output terms before public redistribution.
