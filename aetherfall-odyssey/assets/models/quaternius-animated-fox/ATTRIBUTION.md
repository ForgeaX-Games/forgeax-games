## Intake facts

- 3,752 primitive vertices and 1,848 triangles across five material primitives.
- `NORMAL`, `JOINTS_0`, and `WEIGHTS_0` are present on every primitive.
- 51-joint skin.
- 12 embedded animations: Attack, Death, Eating, Gallop, Gallop Jump, Idle variants, hit reactions,
  Jump To Idle, and Walk.
- Five physically based color materials; no image textures or tangents.
- One embedded data-URI buffer; no external file dependency.

This is a legally clean and technically stronger replacement candidate for the previous no-normal
Khronos Fox. It remains stylized and must still pass real Studio material, animation, and blind visual
quality gates before it can be accepted.
