# 一条街是否取代巷厨厅三段地理

## Parent

[从白盒到如龙式雨夜切片](../MAP.md)

## Question

The destination names **one street** and **one gunfight**. The old spec is an L-alley, kitchen, banquet hall, and coat standoff. Does the Vertical Slice collapse to a single street combat beat (maybe with interior doorways as set dressing), or keep three combat rooms behind a street-scale facade? This decision sets geography before Presentation work.

## Blocked by

None - can start immediately

## Resolution

The Vertical Slice collapses to **one high-density Hong Kong rain street and one
complete gunfight**. The old alley → kitchen → banquet hall sequence is not retained
as three separately authored combat rooms.

Doorways, shopfronts, a covered recess, or a glimpse into an interior may remain as
street set dressing and tactical cover, but they do not become additional levels.
The saved environment, lighting, character, animation, camera, audio, and QA budget
is concentrated on making this one street credible.

The existing three-segment FSM remains evidence and reusable rules material, not the
new geography contract. The replacement spec will define the Investigation/dialogue,
gunfight, and Aftermath as beats occurring on or immediately attached to this street.
